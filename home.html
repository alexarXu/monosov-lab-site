The Laboratory of

We study how the brain perceives, learns, decides, and explores in an uncertain world.

Combining neurobiology, machine learning, and theory, we translate what we learn into computational psychiatry and new algorithms, with the aim of improving health and building machine intelligence that better serves human goals.
